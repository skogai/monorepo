# Current work and next steps

The five roadmap priorities are: isolated mise runner; accessible mise documentation; composed environments and MCP; a reusable environment/PATH plugin; and a mise ecosystem workflow. Marketplace management follows later.

The current wiki implementation is authorized separately by the owner on 2026-11-11. It does not complete the isolation milestone. Next: review the wiki PR and verify installation on another machine. Fresh-checkout reproducibility remains blocked by workstation configuration.

Accepted tool choices are documented in [decisions](decisions.md). Update this snapshot alongside doc/current-work.md whenever the active work changes.
