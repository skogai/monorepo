# Accepted decisions and open questions

ADR 0001 accepts mise, T3 Code, herdr, Git, and Arch Linux with Omarchy workstations as starting tools. Prefer mise-installable tools before custom tooling.

Research proposals are not decisions. No language, database, ticket tracker, encryption mechanism, hosting provider, or marketplace client is selected here. ADR numbering needs resolution because the accepted record reuses 0001.

This wiki summarizes decisions; the ADR directory remains authoritative.
